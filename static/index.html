Optimizer random search · train / test split

Every combination is backtested over the whole period with all fees. The period is split into an older train part and a newer test part. The score is the R per month of the weaker part, divided by the square root of the deepest drawdown (in units of 10 R) — a set only ranks high if it worked in both parts and did not bleed deep. APPLY writes a set into the paper trader (it is used from the next candle on).
